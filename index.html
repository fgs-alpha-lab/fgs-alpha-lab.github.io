FGS Intelligence Engine (the engine) works in three cycles, always in this order. It never touches the money or the system: it only reads, measures and proposes.

Cycle 1

Replays every recorded day.

It goes back through every minute and explains why there were no signals, or where the system failed internally.

Cycle 2

Tests hypotheses without risking money.

A hypothesis is a new strategy or a change to an existing one. It trades in practice mode on days already recorded, and a judge decides.

Cycle 3

Reasons over what was measured.

It reads what cycles 1 and 2 measured and proposes diagnoses, hypotheses, improvements to the system and improvements to the engine itself. Every proposal comes with a prediction that can be checked.

Real example
It found a flaw in its own judge: it was counting market types by mixing practice days with test days. It proposed separating them and predicted the number that should come out.Engine proposals, October 19, 2026.
Always

A person decides.

Nothing applies itself. An approved change is written, tested and verified before it enters the system.

Nothing gets approved by luck.

Before approving a hypothesis, the engine’s judge requires six conditions.

  • At least 50 practice trades.
  • For every $1 it loses, it makes more than $1.40.
  • It repeats it on days it has never seen.Practice days and test days are assigned by a fixed formula based on the date. No one picks them.
  • Luck can’t match it.Its results are shuffled at random 10,000 times. Chance must match it less than 1 time in 100.
  • It works in at least 2 types of market.For example, a day that fell with a lot of movement and a day that rose with little. 10 trades or more in each.
  • If it changes a strategy, it improves something without making anything worse.
